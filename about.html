About Digital Library

A simple place to discover, read, and keep track of the books you love.

Our Story

Digital Library started as a small student project with one simple goal: make it easy for anyone to find a good book and start reading right away. No clutter, no confusing menus — just books, organized and easy to search.

What You Can Do Here

📖 Browse & Search

Explore books by title, author, or category and find something new to read.

⭐ Save Favorites

Keep a personal shelf of books you love, ready whenever you want them.

💻 Read Online

Open and read books directly, without needing to download anything extra.

Built With Care

This project is intentionally kept simple — built by a small team (well, mostly one person and a lot of coffee) who believes reading tools shouldn't be complicated. We're always improving, one small feature at a time.
